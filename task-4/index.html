Color is the visual perception produced by light interacting with the cells in our eyes, specifically the cone cells. It is not an inherent property of matter, but rather how our brain interprets the light absorption, emission, and reflection of objects

Fun Fact About Colores

Colors shape our world, with over 10 million shades distinguishable by the human eye. Blue is the world's most popular color, while red is the first color babies see. Colors influence behavior—yellow and red together can stimulate appetite, while blue often provides a calming effect
